Grammar Points Quick Summary
• zài + Place + Verb Prepositional phrase indicating location MUST be placed BEFORE the verb.
• gēn + Person Used for interactions / accompanying someone (e.g., gēn Lín Nà qù shāngchǎng).
• zhè / nà / nǎ vs zhèr / nàr / nǎr Adding "r" transforms demonstratives into Place/Location words!
• gěi / sòng / zhǎo Double Object Verbs: Subject + Verb + Person (Obj 1) + Item/Money (Obj 2).
• kuài - máo - fēn & jīn (500g) Spoken currency omission rules and fruit weight calculations.

2. Demonstratives Comparison: "zhè/nà/nǎ" vs "zhèr/nàr/nǎr" Pg 153
WITHOUT "r" ➔ Pronouns / Modifiers

Refers to things/people. Requires Measure Word:

  • zhè zhāng guāngpán (this CD)
  • nàge lǎoshī (that teacher)
  • nǎge xuésheng? (which student?)
Formula: zhè / nà / nǎ + Measure Word (+ Noun)
WITH "r" ➔ Location / Place Words!

Adding "r" transforms the word into a Place/Location:

  • zhèr (here) ➔ Wǒmen zài zhèr shàngkè. (We have class here.)
  • nàr (there) ➔ Tā qù nàr yóuyǒng. (He goes there to swim.)
  • nǎr (where) ➔ Nǐ xiànzài zài nǎr? (Where are you now?)
3. Accompaniment with "gēn" (Flexible Time Word Orders) Pg 165

Time words can be placed either BEFORE or AFTER "gēn + Person":

5. Spoken Chinese Currency Hierarchy & Price Omission Pg 158
Spoken Formality Levels:
• Full Form (Formal): RM 3.20 ➔ sān kuài èr máo qián (3.20 yuan)
• Slightly Complete: RM 3.20 ➔ sān kuài èr máo
• Daily Spoken (Omission): RM 3.20 ➔ sān kuài èr
💡 Omission Rule: When "máo" or "fēn" is at the end, the word "máo / máo qián" or "fēn / fēn qián" can be omitted.
6. Weight Unit "jīn" Math Conversion Pg 158
1 jīn = 500 grams (0.5 kg)

sān jīn píngguǒ
= 3 × 500g
= 1500g (1.5 kg)
liǎng jīn pútao
= 2 × 500g
= 1000g (1.0 kg)

Text 1 Breakdown: Likes & CD Shopping Pg 152-153
Key Lines Breakdown (Lines 1–11):
• Dàwéi, nǐ zài zhèr mǎi shénme? (Asking place & action: Subject + zài + Place + Verb + Object)
• Wǒ bù cháng lái zhèr. Xīngqītiān wǒ chángcháng gēn Lín Nà qù xiǎo shāngchǎng. (Frequency + Accompaniment structure)
• Zhè zhāng hěn hǎo, shì Liáng Zhù, hěn yǒumíng. (Demonstrative + MW + Adjective)
• Yǒu, zài nàr mǎi. Gēn wǒ lái, wǒ yě mǎi běnzi. (Using "nàr" for location; "gēn wǒ lái" follow me)
Text 2 Breakdown: Language Problems & Fruit Bargaining Pg 154-155
Key Lines Breakdown (Lines 1–18):
• Duìbuqǐ, wǒ shì wèn: Zhège Hànyǔ zěnme shuō? (Clarifying intention: "wǒ shì wèn...")
• À, nín shì wàiguó rén! Nín zài nǎr gōngzuò? (Exclamation "À" 4th tone)
• Yì jīn píngguǒ duōshao qián? ➔ Yì jīn sān kuài èr máo qián. (Asking/answering unit prices)
• Yígòng shísì kuài qián. Wǒ zài sòng nín yí ge píngguǒ. (Total amount + Double Object verb "sòng")
• Hǎo, nín gěi wǒ èrshí kuài qián, wǒ zhǎo nín liù kuài. (Payment & change: "gěi" + "zhǎo")

4. Change Statements into Questions with Interrogative Pronouns Pg 161
Learned Interrogative Pronouns (疑问代词):
